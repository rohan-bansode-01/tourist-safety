How SafeTour Works

A seamless integration of technology and safety protocols

1

Registration & Digital ID Creation

Tourists register at entry points with verified documents to receive their blockchain-based Digital Tourist ID.

2

App Installation & Configuration

Tourists download the SafeTour app, set preferences, and optionally enable real-time tracking for family.

3

Real-Time Monitoring & Alerts

The system continuously monitors location, behavior patterns, and zone restrictions, sending alerts when needed.

4

Incident Response & Resolution

In case of emergencies, authorities are immediately alerted with precise location and tourist information for rapid response.
